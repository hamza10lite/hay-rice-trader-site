Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What the app does

HAY RICE TRADER is a desktop application that runs on your computer. All your business records (invoices, customers, suppliers, stock, payments, expenses) are stored locally on your computer. We do not run any server that receives or stores your business data.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to (a) know your email address, to show which account is connected, and (b) create and manage only the backup files that the app itself creates in your Google Drive (the "drive.file" permission). The app cannot see or open any other file in your Google Drive.

Your backups are uploaded directly from your computer to your own Google Drive. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party. Your sign-in token is stored on your computer in encrypted form. You can sign out at any time from Settings → Google Drive → Logout, and you can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature, is not sold, is not shared with others, and is not used for advertising.

Data we collect

We do not collect, track or sell personal data. The app does not include advertising or analytics.

Your control

You can delete your backups from your Google Drive at any time, and you can delete the app's local data by uninstalling the app and removing its data folder.
