HAY RICE TRADER

HAY RICE TRADER is a Windows desktop application for wholesale rice traders. It handles purchasing, stock, billing and invoices, customer and supplier accounts, payments, expenses, profit & loss and reports.

Your business data is stored on your own computer. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app can save backup copies of your data in your own Google Drive.
